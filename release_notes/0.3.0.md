Fixes from the first long runs of 0.2.0, a simpler first setup, and eighteen new bots.

If a bot of your own uses Open Skeleton Key Door, fill in its From Zone again after
updating.

Runs:

- A player with no mana and no potions buys potions and rejoins the team.
- Dungeon Farmer bots use or buy a potion when the hub has no mana wisp nearby, as is the case in Lemuria.
- A run that fails and recovers the same way three times in a row stops, and says which
  steps repeated.
- A lost connection during a relog relaunches the game.
- Group formation clicks Invite to Group reliably, and closes the Crowns shop when it
  opens.
- Relog works while the dungeon exit prompt is showing.
- Collect Wisps waits up to the Safety Delay for wisps to appear after a fight.
- Closing the backpack, the spellbook, the potion shop or the zone list is retried until
  the window closes.
- A team relog logs players in one at a time.
- Enter Dungeon fails, and the team recovers, when a player outside the team enters with
  it.
- A skeleton key door takes the whole team through.

Combat:

- Fights spend less time between rounds.
- An enchanted card that doesn't cast is cast again, and each spell targets the enemies
  present when it is cast.
- Drops are read after every fight.
- A bot starts without a pause.
- A game window that stops answering holds up only its own game.

Setup and launching:

- Setup says why it asks for your Wizard101 password, and that accounts are Wizard101
  accounts.
- With one account, setup skips the team questions.
- Quicksell is off until you turn it on.
- Players are named Player 1, Player 2 and so on.
- On the launch screen, a click selects an account or profile. Launch, Enter or a
  double-click launches it, and the last one launched is selected.
- Setup says that a game in BartleBot's window is still yours to play.
- A run warns at the start when the game's Dblclick Go To Friend option is on, since it
  stops teleports to friends.
- When the wiki asks for a security check during an import, a window opens for you to
  pass it.

Editing bots:

- Done saves and leaves Edit mode. Save saves and stays in Edit mode. A dot on Save and a
  * in the title mark unsaved changes.
- Record fills in a skeleton key door.
- Speed-authoring goes up to 2x, and is safe to leave on while you switch characters.
- The game view shows a pointer only over what you can click.
- The window opens maximized on high-resolution screens without freezing.

New bots: Jacques the Scratcher, Shirataki Temple, Tomugawa the Evil, Zarathax, Caoranach
the Fire-Spitter, Morganthe, Galleries, Tartarus, Fellspawn, Dasein Duelist, Don Manzana,
Ra, Aethyr Elemental, Aphrodite II, Hollow Mountain, Pagoda of Harmony, Upper Halls, and
the Sinbad & the Iron Sultan gauntlet. Updated: Castle Darkmoor, Graveyard, Barkingham
Palace's State Wing, Heretical Academy and the Aquila bots.

This release includes security updates.
