BartleBot works with the game again after the November 19 update.

The November 19 update:

- BartleBot connects to the game after the update, and a launch skips the loading screens
  again.
- Clicks land in a game window whether or not the mouse is over it.
- The free camera button on a player's card switches the camera.

Launching and teams:

- A launch logs in and takes Play again after the November 16 update.
- A player teleporting to its group's leader uses the game's Teleport to Leader when it can,
  which takes two clicks and opens no window.

This release also has everything 0.3.1 brought:

- BartleBot connects to the game after the Ahmarra update.
- Ahmarra is on the World Gate list, and Bale Flotilla is among the zones.
- Challenge Mode thresholds count levels up to 190, so Darkmoor's dungeons scale a level
  190 player down.
- The Connections card names the condition an edge waits on, where it used to say
  "always".
- The Log tab's Result line names the condition that chose the next step.
- In a Challenge Mode dungeon, deck import and apply are skipped, and Handle Potions uses
  no potions, since the game allows neither there.
- Quicksell stops paging once it has passed every page the backpack and jewel pouch could
  fill.
- Accounts and launch profiles added in BartleBuddy show up in BartleBot without a restart.
