The first update to the beta, made from what its first testers ran into. Thank you to
everyone who tried 0.1.0 and said what went wrong.

Combat strategies:

- Adding a duel strategy is now two clear steps: choose or make the strategy, then choose
  the fights it is for. Nothing is added until you press Add, and the new card is
  highlighted so you can see where it went.
- Each fight's card has a Remove button, which hands its fights back to the default card
  and keeps the strategy itself.
- The support strategies now cast prisms.
- A new Default AoE Spam Hitter enchants ahead, then hits with an enchanted AoE every round.

Recovering a run:

- When the game crashes mid-fight, BartleBot gets the team back into the fight, or logs
  everyone out together, instead of leaving players stranded.
- The relog node no longer relogs a player who has only just logged in.

Clearer while a bot runs:

- The Combat and Options tabs are locked while a bot runs, so nothing changes under it.
- Validate Bot always says what it found, even when that is nothing.
- The game view's toolbar shows a pointer only over buttons you can press, and a disabled
  button says why.
- The log's minimum level now applies to lines already shown, and starts at Warning.

New:

- Delete a bot from inside the app.
- Pan the bot canvas by dragging with the middle mouse button.
- The bot search shows which folder it is limited to.
- The window has a visible bottom edge.
- The Lord Nightshade bot's boss fight is listed on the Combat tab, so it can have a
  strategy of its own.
- Travel from the Oasis to Kembaalung Village uses newly recorded coordinates.
