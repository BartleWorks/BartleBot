# The bot library

The library lists every bot you can run. This page shows how to find the one you want, what Official means, and how to get a copy of your own when you want to change one.

## Open the library

While no bot is open, the bot canvas shows the library. With a bot open, use **File › Browse Bot Library…** or **File › Close Bot** to get back to it. **File › Open Recent** lists the bots you opened last.

Select a bot's card to open it.

## Find a bot

The list on the left narrows the library to one place:

- **Recent** lists the bots you opened last. It's first once you've opened one.
- **All** lists every bot.
- **The world and activity rows**, such as Wizard City, Crafting or Housing Gauntlet, list the bots for that world or activity, in the game's own order. A bot outside any of them is under **Other**.

The bar above the cards narrows it further:

- **Rewards** and **Activities** filter by tag. Rewards are what a run gets you, such as Gear, Reagents or Treasure Cards. Activities are what the bot does, such as Boss, Dungeon, Gauntlet or Collecting. Each tag you pick shows as a chip under the bar. Select a chip to remove it.
- **Source** filters by where a bot comes from: **Official**, or **Custom** for your own.
- **The search box** finds a bot by its name, its author, or a drop. A drop that matches is named on the bot's card.

## Read a bot's card

Each card shows the bot's name and version, its tags, and where it comes from. Under them are its description, its author, and how many drops it lists.

A card can also say:

- **Copy of the Official** and a bot's name: this is your own copy of that Official bot.
- **2 setup steps left**: a bot you made from a template that isn't ready to run yet.
- **Template update available**: the template your bot was made from has a new version. Opening the bot offers the changes.

Open the bot and read its description before you run it. If it names a place to start, or a level, take your wizards there first.

## Official bots and your own

Official bots come with BartleBot. Each one is tested in game, and an app update can improve it. An Official bot can't be changed, renamed or deleted, so every update reaches it.

You don't need a copy to run one. Its options and its combat setup are yours to change from the **Options** and **Combat** tabs, and BartleBot keeps them in your settings, apart from the bot. [Options and Settings](options-and-settings.md) has how.

Your own bots are the ones you make with [**New Bot**](../making/new-bot-and-templates.md#new-bot), copy, or [import](../making/sharing.md#import). Right-click one's card to **Export…** it as a share file, or **Delete Bot…**.

## Make your own copy of an Official bot

To change an Official bot's steps, make your own copy:

1. Open the Official bot.
2. Select the pencil in the bot canvas's toolbar.
3. BartleBot asks to **Make my own copy**. Change the name if you like, and select **Make my own copy**.

The copy opens in Edit mode, ready to change. It keeps the option values and Bot Combat Strategies you set for the Official bot, and its card says which bot it was copied from. The Official bot stays as it is.

Your copy is yours from then on. When an update improves the Official bot, your copy stays as you made it.
