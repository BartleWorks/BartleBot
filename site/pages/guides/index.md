# Guides

Each guide walks through one task, with the app open beside it. To set up BartleBot and finish a first run, start with [Getting started](../getting-started.md). When something goes wrong, [Troubleshooting](../troubleshooting.md) starts from what you see.

## Running bots

- [The bot library](running/library.md): finding a bot, Official bots and your own, and making your own copy.
- [Running a bot](running/running-a-bot.md): what's checked before a run, watching it, pausing and stopping, and what ends it.
- [Options and Settings](running/options-and-settings.md): where an option's value comes from, setting your own, and which Settings tab holds what.
- [Quicksell and keep lists](running/quicksell.md): what is sold and when, and the ways to keep what you want.
- [Accounts, clients and realms](running/accounts-clients-and-realms.md): saving accounts, launching games, running a team, and realms.
- [When something goes wrong in a run](running/when-something-goes-wrong.md): crashes, relogs, fights no step started, and when BartleBot stops.
- [Safety](running/safety.md): what running a bot risks for your account, Safety Delay, and the warnings before a run.
- [PvP](running/pvp.md): what BartleBot won't automate, and what happens if a run reaches it.

## Making bots

Read these in order to build a first bot from a blank one: it loops, and routes its own failure.

- [New Bot and templates](making/new-bot-and-templates.md): starting a bot, filling in a template's setup steps, taking its updates, and making templates of your own.
- [The canvas](making/the-canvas.md): Edit mode, adding and connecting nodes, a node's page, recording from the game, and the Entity and UI Tree browsers.
- [Edges and failure](making/edges-and-failure.md): what a result with no edge does, the recovery state, stopping the bot on purpose, and showing failure edges.
- [Variables and conditions](making/variables-and-conditions.md): edges that read an option or a variable, and setting and watching variables.
- [Subgraphs](making/subgraphs.md): using one, making one, choosing its inputs, and what changing one warns about.
- [Sharing](making/sharing.md): exporting and importing share files, and what does and doesn't travel.
